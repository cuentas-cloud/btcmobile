The story

Why we pay you in Bitcoin.

In 2008, the financial system broke. A nine-page paper proposed a system that didn't require trust. Since then, Bitcoin has been adopted by individuals, public companies and even governments. The same reasons apply to the bill you pay every month.

Your phone bill is one of your most predictable expenses. We think part of it should come back to you as the asset you believe in.
